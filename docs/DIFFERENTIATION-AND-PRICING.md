# Differentiation & Pricing Strategy

**Audience:** founder. **Purpose:** lock the category we play in, the buyer we serve, the wedge, the packaging ladder, and the pricing — explicitly designed *not* to enter the measurement price war that has already collapsed to $29/mo.

---

## 0. The one strategic insight (read this first)

> "Most execution problems are actually strategy problems." — Shreyas Doshi

The entire GEO category is racing toward the **same ~7 metrics**. When everyone measures the same thing, measurement becomes a commodity and price collapses — **Otterly is already at $29/mo**. Profound has the brand, funding, and head start. **We will lose a measurement war.**

April Dunford's rule: *don't position into a strength you can't win — position into a weakness the leader can't fix without breaking their own model.* The leader's weakness is **structural, not a feature gap**: measurement companies cannot also be the ones who *fix* your brand, because fixing requires (a) on-brand content generation, (b) real logged-in AI capture, and (c) being accountable to an **outcome** instead of a dashboard. That is our entire game: **the closed loop.**

## 2. Our positioning (Dunford)

**Competitive alternatives (what the buyer does today):** not "no GEO tool." The real status quo is **"measured but stuck"** — they buy Profound/Peec/Otterly, get a dashboard full of red, and have no idea what to do next, so the report rots, or they hand it to an agency that takes 6 weeks to ghost-write generic content that **dilutes the brand**.

**Category — pick this, not the leader's:**

| | Measurement crowd (Profound, Peec, Otterly, Athena, Scrunch) | **CanMarket** |
|---|---|---|
| **Category** | AI Visibility **Monitoring** | **AI Answer Optimization** (closed loop) |
| **Output** | Dashboard / score | On-brand fixes shipped + score moved |
| **Capture** | API-based, sanitized | Real logged-in browser capture (what a *human* sees) |
| **Content** | None — "here's your gap" | Brand-Memory regenerated, on-brand (Style Genome™) |
| **Accountability** | A number | An **outcome** (we re-measure and prove the lift) |

> ❌ Do **not** name our category "GEO Monitoring / AI Visibility Tracking." That's the leader's category — entering it makes us "Profound but cheaper," the race to the bottom. ✅ Name it **"GEO Performance / AI Answer Optimization."**

**ICP (who cares a lot):** mid-market **DTC / brand-led companies** that have a brand worth protecting and already feel the AI-answer gap — the same profile as our existing brand-memory clients (Gimme5, Boba Pop, Modern Shade, Sakura, Winona, limone), plus brand-conscious marketing teams who would *never* accept generic ghost-written content. They are "measured but stuck" and they care about brand integrity more than about a cheaper dashboard.

**The wedge (narrowest entry point):** the **one-off GEO Audit** that doesn't just show red — it shows the **real human-visible answer**, names the exact on-brand fix, and is priced as a paid diagnostic. It lands the account, proves the closed-loop story in one deliverable, and creates the natural upsell to monitoring → fix-as-service.

**Value those enable:** (1) you see what your customer *actually* sees, not the sanitized API answer; (2) you get fixes, not homework; (3) the fixes are *on-brand*, so performance doesn't break the brand; (4) you can prove the score moved (multi-run averaging gives the variance band that makes a real lift distinguishable from noise).

Goodie is the nearest comp at ~$495–$780 but writes generic content with no brand memory; agencies cost 2–4× and take 6 weeks. We are faster than the agency and on-brand unlike Goodie — and we charge for the *outcome*, the one thing measurement companies structurally cannot bill for.

## 5. Anti-race-to-the-bottom logic (why we don't get dragged to $29)

1. **Don't sell the commodity.** Measurement is the commodity (already $29). We sell **the outcome** (answer moved, on-brand). Different category, different budget line, different buyer expectation. A buyer comparing us to Otterly on price is in the wrong category — our positioning re-frames the comparison to *agency retainers*, where we're the cheaper, faster, on-brand option.

2. **Price the wedge as a diagnostic, not a trial.** The Audit is **$500–$1,500 one-off**, not a free dashboard. Charging for the diagnostic filters for serious brands and immediately signals "consulting outcome," not "tool."

3. **Anchor the recurring tiers to the right neighbor.** GEO Watch sits *at* Peec/Otterly (even comparison, we win on honesty) so we never look like the expensive monitor. GEO Optimize anchors *against agencies* ($2K–$8K) so we always look like the cheaper, faster, on-brand outcome — never the expensive dashboard.

4. **Bundle the moat into the high tier only.** Brand Memory + browser-real capture + before/after proof live in **Optimize**. They are not unbundled into cheap tiers, so the value that justifies premium pricing can't be cherry-picked at the floor price.

5. **Let the leader's model be its cage.** Profound/Peec/Otterly cannot add on-brand fixing without (a) building a brand-memory content engine, (b) moving from API to real browser capture (their entire cost model is API-based), and (c) accepting outcome accountability instead of dashboard SLAs. Any one of those breaks their business model. So the more we win on closed-loop, the harder it is for them to follow — that's a durable moat, not a feature lead.

**Bottom line:** we never quote against a $29 dashboard. We quote against a $3,000 agency retainer and a brand-dilution risk — and we win both.
